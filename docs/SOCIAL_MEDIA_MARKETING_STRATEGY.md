## 4. Platform Strategy

### 4.1 Instagram – Primary Driver (Priority #1)

**Why:** Tourism discovery and booking decisions begin on Instagram Reels. The algorithm rewards short, authentic, visually rich coastal content.

**Content mix:**

| Content Type | Frequency | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Reel – Experience teasers | 3x / week | Top of funnel — discovery & desire |
| Reel – Behind the scenes | 1x / week | Trust & personality building |
| Reel – Guest video UGC | 1x / week | Social proof / authenticity |
| Carousel – Trip detail | 1x / week | Mid-funnel — educate & qualify |
| Static post – Brand / announcement | 1x / week | Nurture & update |
| Instagram Stories | Daily | Engagement, polls, booking CTAs |

**Monthly Reel content plan (4 per trip):**

| Trip | Hook (first 3 sec) | Length |
|---|---|---|
| Fort Jesus Hop-On Hop-Off | Drone pull-back from boat onto Fort Jesus walls | 45–60s |
| Sunset Sailing | "Mombasa's golden hour is not from the beach — it's from here" | 30–45s |
| Snorkelling Reef | B-roll of fish + guest's genuine "wow" reaction | 30s |
| Creek Safaris / Mangrove | Glass-bottom boat reveal — calm, hypnotic | 45s |
| Private Charter | Inside perspective: handle a private party on the deck | 30s |
| Birthdays & Anniversaries | Cake reveal / toast moment onboard | 30s |

**Reels strategy rules:**
- Hook in first 3 seconds — immediate visual reward (ocean shot, drone reveal, smiling guest)
- Narrative arc in first 8 seconds — "What is this?" → "That soon resolves"
- Text overlays on every Reel, not relying on audio
- End slate always reads: "Book in bio | WhatsApp +254 708 485 978"
- Use trending Kenyan/Travel audio whenever appropriate
- Upload at optimal times: Weekdays 12:00–13:00 EAT and 18:00–20:00 EAT; Weekends 10:00–12:00 EAT

## 5. Social Proof Strategy

Social proof is the single most impactful lever we have. International travellers trust peer content far more than branded posts.

### 5.1 Instagram Testimonial Reels

Every guest interaction is an opportunity to produce content:

1. **Ask guests at the end of every trip:** "Would you mind if I record a 10-second video of you saying one word about the trip? We post it on our page to help others book."
2. **Record:** Guest's face, short authentic clip no more than 15 seconds
3. **Post:** As a Reel with caption: "Just off the boat — this is what a Fort Jesus trip actually looks like 👀"
4. **Result:** Authenticity generates more clicks and bookings than polished production
5. **Follow up:** "Happy you loved it Kiran! Let us know if you return to Mombasa ❤️" — reply publicly to each testimonial

### 5.2 Google Reviews Campaign

Every booking confirmation email/SMS should end with a direct link to leave a Google review. Goal: 2–4 new reviews per week.

### 5.3 Website Testimonial Integration

Each trip page on the website should have a live-reviews section — quotes pulled from Google and Instagram.

## 6. Engagement Strategy

Organic algorithms reward reciprocal engagement. We will commit to the following:

1. **Reply rate: 100% within 1 hour**
   - Every comment (DM too) gets a response with specific, personalised content — not a generic copy-paste
   - Booking questions move to WhatsApp immediately

2. **Community-first commenting**
   - Every local tourism colleague ~ leave authentic gratitude comments
   - Engage with Kenyan / beach / coastal / safari accounts daily

3. **Follower activation**
   - 2x / month: caption a trip photo asking followers "Tag someone who needs this on their bucket list"
   - 1x / month: caption a reel asking "Drop your biggest worry about a boat trip and we'll answer"
   - Both tactics spike algorithm signals and build trust

4. **User-Generated Content (UGC) collection**
   - Create a branded hashtag: #BluePineappleMombasa
   - Encourage tags in booking confirmations
   - Repost best 1–2 UGC pieces per week

## 10. Influencer & Partnership Strategy

Tourism grows through trusted recommendations. We will build a program of local benefit exchange, not just paid placements.

| Type | Scope | Agreement |
|---|---|---|
| **Micro influencer** (2K–10K followers, Mombasa / Kenya) | 1–2 visits per quarter, trip exchange + small stipend | Post 2–3 Reels + 2–3 posts across 30 days post-visit |
| **Nano influencer** (500–2K, local families, photography) | 1 visit per month, free trip for family | 1 reel + 1 static, tag @bluepineapple in their feed |
| **Hotel / concierge partner** | Ongoing, every hotel lounge | Concierge receives 10–15% commission on bookings; they proactively recommend Blue Pineapple to hotel guests |
| **Travel agent collaboration** | Nairobi-based | White-label package for groups; broker commission structure |

**Incentive model:** Influencers receive two free experiences of their choice (up to KES 20,000 value per visit) in exchange for authentic content. No overly scripted reviews — we offer the experience and the right to share freely; creative quality should be their own.

- [ ] Caption opens with an emotional hook (not a generic description)

- [ ] Correct aspect ratio

- [ ] Correct caption length per platform rules

- [ ] Scheduled during peak engagement hours for platform

## 14. Booking Funnel Activation

Social → WhatsApp → Confirmed booking

```
Instagram/TikTok Reel with Trip hook
    ↓
    "DM 'BOOK' to check availability"
        ↓
    Client replies with trip, date, guests
        ↓
    Blue Pineapple responds with:
    – Availability check
    – Pricing + discount info
    – WhatsApp booking link / Google Form
        ↓
    Guest books → Booking confirmed → Trip email/SMS sent
```

**Automation note:** A future enhancement is an AI chatbot on WhatsApp Business — available FAQs answered automatically 24/7, reducing manual response burden. This builds on the existing website and streamlines the booking pipeline.
