# Google Search Console Demo Script for Clients

---

## Part 1: What is SEO? (1-2 minutes)

**Key points to explain:**

> "SEO stands for **Search Engine Optimization**. In simple terms, it's about making your website appear higher in Google search results when people look for services like yours.
>
> When someone searches 'boat trips Mombasa' or 'Fort Jesus tour', we want BluePineapple to appear on the first page of Google. That's what SEO does - it helps potential customers find you online."

**Visual to show:** 
- Search for "boat trips Mombasa" on Google
- Point out where BluePineapple would ideally appear

---

## Part 2: Google Search Console Overview (2-3 minutes)

**Say to client:**

> "Google Search Console is a free tool from Google that shows us how our website performs in search. Think of it as a dashboard - it tells us:
> - How many people found us on Google
> - Which pages are indexed (Google knows about them)
> - What search terms people use to find us
> - If there are any issues preventing us from appearing in search"

---

## Part 3: Where to Check - Step by Step

### Step 1: Indexing > Pages
**URL:** https://search.google.com/search-console

> "This shows us which pages Google has indexed. Currently we have 1 page indexed, but after our recent updates, we expect all main pages to be indexed:
> - Homepage
> - /trips (all experiences)
> - /trips/fort-jesus-trip (main product)
> - /boats (fleet)
> - /boats/setting-sons
> - /boats/hunky-dory
> - /gallery
> - /contact"

### Step 2: Search Results > Performance
> "This shows:
> - **Impressions** - How many times your site appeared in search results
> - **Clicks** - How many people actually clicked through to your site
> - **Average position** - Where you rank (lower number = better)
> - **CTR (Click Through Rate)** - Percentage of people who clicked"

### Step 3: Sitemaps
> "We've submitted a sitemap - it's like a table of contents that helps Google find all our pages faster."

---

## Part 4: What to Show the Client After Visiting the Site

**Important:** After YOU visit the new pages on the live site, Google will start crawling them. Here's what to check:

### 1. Wait 24-48 hours after deployment
### 2. Visit Google Search Console > Indexing > Pages
### 3. Look for:
   - ✅ "Valid" status
   - ✅ More pages showing under "Valid page indexing"
   - ✅ Error-free status

## Demo Talking Points

**When showing the dashboard:**

1. **Point to the verified property:** "This confirms we own the website"

2. **Show Indexing section:** "This tells Google which pages exist. We're working to get all our pages indexed"

3. **Show Performance:** "This is where we see traffic from Google - impressions, clicks, etc."

4. **Explain the "Duplicate without user-selected canonical" issue:**
   > "You may see an error here - it's a common issue when www and non-www versions exist. We've fixed this, and it should resolve as Google re-crawls our pages."

## Key Terms Glossary

| Term | Simple Meaning |
|------|---------------|
| **Indexing** | Google adding your page to its database |
| **Crawling** | Google visiting your pages to check them |
| **Sitemap** | A list of all your pages for Google |
| **Canonical** | The preferred version of a URL (www vs non-www) |
| **Impressions** | Times your site showed in search |
| **Clicks** | Times people clicked to visit your site |
| **Ranking** | Your position in search results (1-10 = page 1) |
